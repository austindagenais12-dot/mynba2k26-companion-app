# Second Draft

Second Draft is an original, choice-driven mobile life simulator made in Godot 4. Every life is built from a reproducible procedural seed and follows a different character from birth through family, childhood, education, sports, work, relationships, assets, retirement, and a final life summary.

This project is genre-inspired but contains no BitLife code, writing, artwork, branding, or other proprietary assets.

## Play loop

- Tap **Age +1** to move forward one year.
- Begin with a generated name, identity, birthplace, household, parents, siblings, childhood history, attributes, and animated portrait DNA.
- Make choices in context-aware events assembled dynamically from the character's age, family, education, job, finances, health, relationships, and sport. Important decisions become memories that can return years later as chained consequences.
- Build six core attributes: health, happiness, smarts, confidence, discipline, and reputation.
- Study, work, build relationships, purchase assets, and manage yearly income and expenses.
- Search 5,120 careers across 40 real-world sectors, workplaces, and qualification levels.
- Play a role-specific, three-round realistic work shift for every one of the 5,120 careers, mixing professional judgement with interactive ordering challenges.
- Try out for one of 36 sports during youth or school, complete position-aware training drills and seasons, manage injuries and rehabilitation, pursue scholarships, and progress into salary-linked professional contracts and retirement.
- Complete college, university, graduate, professional, doctoral, or medical study paths.
- Drag to scroll every long screen, keep each tab's position, and automatically reveal text fields above the Android keyboard.
- Copy build, save-schema, platform, and life diagnostics from the Assets tab, with device installation help available in-game.
- Your complete history is recorded in the Life timeline and saved locally.

## Development

Open `project.godot` with Godot 4.7 or newer. The Android preset exports a portrait ARM64 debug APK.
