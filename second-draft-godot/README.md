# Second Draft

Second Draft is an original, choice-driven mobile life simulator made in Godot 4. It follows a character from birth through childhood, education, work, relationships, assets, retirement, and a final life summary.

This project is genre-inspired but contains no BitLife code, writing, artwork, branding, or other proprietary assets.

## Play loop

- Tap **Age +1** to move forward one year.
- Make choices in age-appropriate events.
- Build six core attributes: health, happiness, smarts, confidence, discipline, and reputation.
- Study, work, build relationships, purchase assets, and manage yearly income and expenses.
- Your complete history is recorded in the Life timeline and saved locally.

## Development

Open `project.godot` with Godot 4.7 or newer. The Android preset exports a portrait ARM64 debug APK.
